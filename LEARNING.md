On a phone the three cards stack. Colours come from our tokens (sage dots, moss headings), not the purple in the mock-up: `CLAUDE.md` §1.

### The API change you're consuming

Every `summaryPoints[i]` now has a `section`: `"objective" | "scope" | "qualifications"`, or `null` on summaries written before topics existed. The frontend:

- **narrows** it in `toTor` (`src/api/tors.ts`, already wired to call your `toSummaryPoint`): unknown → `null`;
- **groups** points into three cards, always in that order, **always all three**;
- **leaves out** `null`-section points. Filing them under a guessed topic would put words in the document's mouth.

### Two different "nothing" states (the subtle part)

| Situation | Show |
|---|---|
| a topic has no points | that card says **"ยังไม่มีข้อมูลส่วนนี้จาก Procurement"** |
| **no** topic has points (summary not run yet, or only old rows) | **one** message, *"ยังไม่มีการสรุปสาระสำคัญ…"*, and **no cards** |

Why: three cards each saying "the documents don't cover this" would be false when the truth is "we haven't summarized it yet". A test checks this.

## Steps

### Step 1: `toSummaryPoint`

**File:** `src/lib/summarySections.ts`. Keep `point.section` only if it's one of `TOR_SUMMARY_SECTIONS`; anything else (missing, `null`, `"advice"`, a number) becomes `null`. Everything else passes through unchanged. `oneOf()` in `src/api/tors.ts` shows the Set-narrowing pattern.

✅ `bun test src/lib/summarySections.test.ts -t "step 1"`, and `checklist/92` → "step 1".

### Step 2: `groupSummary`

Same file. Always three groups, in `TOR_SUMMARY_SECTIONS` order, each `{ section, points }`, with points kept in their original order. Mapping over `TOR_SUMMARY_SECTIONS` gives you the order **and** the empty cards for free. `null`-section points land in no group.

✅ `bun test src/lib/summarySections.test.ts` is fully green.

### Step 3: `SummarySections`

**File:** `src/components/tor/SummarySections.tsx`. The props are typed and the body returns `null`; the TODO lists the structure.

- All groups empty → one `<p>` with `labels.notGenerated`, no cards.
- Otherwise a grid (`grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-3`) of three `<section>`s. Each has `data-section`, and an `aria-labelledby` pointing at its `<h3 id="summary-…">`, so a screen reader announces "ขอบเขตงาน, region".
- Inside a card: a `<ul>` of points (text in `<p lang="th">`, which tells the browser and screen reader which language to use), or `labels.empty`.
- Take the bullet-dot and text classes from the old flat list in the detail page. Same look, new layout.

✅ `bun test src/components/tor` (6 tests).

### Step 4: wire the detail page

**File:** `src/app/(public)/tor/[id]/page.tsx`

1. Replace `const summaryPoints = tor.summaryPoints ?? []` with `const summaryGroups = groupSummary(tor.summaryPoints ?? [])`.
2. Replace the whole "Extracted details" `<section>` (the flat list) with:
   - the eyebrow `t.summaryEyebrow` (`font-mono text-xs tracking-widest text-sage-600 uppercase`, the house eyebrow; see `CLAUDE.md` §5)
   - the heading `t.summaryHeading` as an `<h2>`
   - `<SummarySections groups={summaryGroups} labels={{ titles: t.summarySections, empty: t.summarySectionEmpty, notGenerated: t.summaryPointsEmpty }} />`
   - **keep** `t.summaryProvenanceNote` below it when any card has points. The points are model-written, and the page has to say so.
3. The cards are white on the page's paper background, so don't wrap them in another white bordered panel (`CLAUDE.md` §3: no cards inside same-coloured panels).

✅ `bun test checklist/92` is fully green. `bun test` is fully green. tsc, lint and build pass.
